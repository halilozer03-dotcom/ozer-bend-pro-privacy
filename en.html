BENDIQ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Türkçe sürüm

Overview

BENDIQ ("the App") is a professional tool for sheet metal bending calculations. We care about our users' privacy. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, why, and how you can delete your account.

Data We Collect

The dimensions, material choices, favourites, calibration values, company name and language preference you enter are stored only in your device's local storage (localStorage) and are not sent to our servers.

The following features are exceptions and are described separately: Sign in with Google (account), Subscriptions and Purchases, Free Trial and AI Measurement Scan.

Sign in with Google and Your Account

You sign in to the App with your Google account so that your free trial and subscription are tied to your account. Signing in creates a user identity on our secure server (Supabase Auth) and stores the following information Google provides: your email address, your name and your Google account identifier. This information is used only to sign you in and to verify your trial and subscription rights; it is not used for advertising or marketing, is not sold and is not shared with third parties. Your password never reaches us.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Subscriptions (monthly or yearly) and PRO purchases from earlier versions are processed by Google Play Billing. To verify your entitlement, our server queries Google Play and stores the following record linked to your account: purchase token, order number, product, validity dates and status (active, cancelled, refunded, etc.). Your payment card details never reach us; they are handled directly by Google Play. You can cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play.

Free Trial Verification

Each Google account gets a 30-day free trial once. To prevent the trial from being taken repeatedly by creating new accounts on the same phone, the device identifier provided by your operating system (Android ID) and the trial start/end dates are stored with the trial record. The trial is shared between Android and desktop: a trial started on the phone continues on the computer with its remaining days, and no second trial is given. If the trial is started on the desktop version (Windows/Linux), the computer's one-way hash (see Desktop Version) is stored instead of the device identifier.

Desktop Version (Windows and Linux)

On the desktop version, the account, trial and subscription are the same as on Android (one account works on both platforms). The differences are:

  • Sign-in: Sign in with Google opens in your computer's default browser; after sign-in the browser returns only to the App on this computer (127.0.0.1). The session token is stored encrypted in your operating system's protected storage (DPAPI on Windows).
  • Payment — Stripe: On the desktop version the BENDIQ PRO subscription is charged through Stripe (on Android through Google Play; both give the same access). Stripe processes your payment and billing details (card, email, country); your card details never reach us. The subscription status is kept on our payment server running on Cloudflare (bendiq-api.ozerlabs.com), linked to your Google account, and contains only: Google account number, Stripe customer number, Stripe subscription number, plan (price code), status, validity date and Stripe notification records (notification number, type, date). Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy. You cancel the subscription and change the payment method in the Stripe customer portal.
  • Device limit: An account can be used on a limited number of computers at the same time. For this, your computer's operating system identifier (Windows MachineGuid / Linux machine-id) is passed through a one-way hash on your computer; only this hash, the platform (Windows/Linux) and the first/last seen dates are sent to the server. The raw identifier never reaches us and cannot be recovered from the hash. When the limit is reached, the App lists your registered computers and you can remove one; when you delete your account, the device records are deleted too.
  • Offline use: The subscription stays valid without internet for at most 7 days; after that the App connects to the internet to verify it again.
  • Files: PDF and DXF files are saved to the folder you choose through the operating system's "Save" dialog; printing uses the operating system's print dialog.
  • Microphone: The desktop version has no voice measurement input; the App does not request microphone permission.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account inside the App: Settings → Delete account. Deletion removes your sign-in identity (email address and name) from our server. Purchase and trial records are kept without your email address for legal (tax/record-keeping) obligations, refund handling and to prevent abuse of the free trial; they are kept for 10 years and then deleted. Local data on your device (dimensions, favourites) is deleted when you uninstall the App.

If you no longer have access to the App, send your deletion request to halilozer03@gmail.com from the Google email address you signed in with, with the subject "BENDIQ account deletion"; it is processed the same way as in-app deletion.

AI Measurement Scan

When you use this feature, the profile photo you take or select is sent through a secure server (Supabase Edge Function) to Google's Gemini AI service to detect the corner points. The photo is used only for this analysis and is not stored permanently on the server. A general usage counter (IP-based, daily, to prevent abuse) is kept during this process; this counter does not contain your personal identity.

Voice Measurement Input

When you use the voice command feature, your speech is processed by your device's operating system / Google speech recognition service to convert it into text. This does not pass through our servers; voice data never reaches us.

Generated Files (PDF and DXF)

PDF technical drawings and DXF flat pattern files you create in the App are generated entirely on your device and are only handled by your device's share/save systems when you choose to share or save them. These files are never seen or stored by us.

Third-Party Services

The App uses only the following services: Supabase (sign-in, subscription and trial verification, AI scan server) , Google (Sign in with Google, Google Play Billing, Google Play Developer API, Gemini) and, on the desktop version, Stripe (payments) and Cloudflare (payment server). It shows no ads and uses no analytics/tracking software (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.).

Because the App is distributed through the Google Play Store and uses Sign in with Google, Google's own privacy policy also applies: policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions

  • File system / sharing: to save and share PDF and DXF files. Used only at your request.
  • Microphone: used only when you start "Voice Measurement Input" and only while you are speaking. Recordings are never sent to our servers or saved on the device; they are passed to the operating system's speech recognition service and converted into text. If you do not grant the permission, the rest of the App works normally.
  • Camera / gallery: only to take or select a photo on the "AI Measurement Scan" screen. The App never takes photos in the background.
  • Internet: for Sign in with Google, subscription and trial verification, AI scan and purchases.

The App does not access location, contacts, call history, SMS or similar data.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for professionals working in the sheet metal bending industry and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy
