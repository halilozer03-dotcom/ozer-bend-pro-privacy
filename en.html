BENDIQ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Türkçe sürüm

Overview

BENDIQ ("the App") is a professional tool for sheet metal bending calculations. We care about our users' privacy. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, why, and how you can delete your account.

Data We Collect

The dimensions, material choices, favourites, calibration values, company name and language preference you enter are stored only in your device's local storage (localStorage) and are not sent to our servers.

The following features are exceptions and are described separately: Sign in with Google (desktop version only), Subscriptions and Purchases, Free Trial and AI Measurement Scan.

Sign in with Google and Your Account

On the phone (Android) there is no sign-in and no account is created. Your phone subscription is recognised from the purchases of the Google Play account that is active on your device (see Subscriptions and Purchases); if you switch Google Play accounts, the App works with that account's purchases. On the phone the App does not receive your email address or name.

You sign in to the desktop (Windows/Linux) version with your Google account; earlier phone versions of the App also used sign-in. Signing in creates a user identity on our secure server (Supabase Auth) and stores the following information Google provides: your email address, your name and your Google account identifier. This information is used only to sign you in and to verify your trial and subscription rights; it is not used for advertising or marketing, is not sold and is not shared with third parties. Your password never reaches us.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Subscriptions (monthly or yearly) and PRO purchases from earlier versions are processed by Google Play Billing. To verify your entitlement, the phone sends the purchase tokens of the Google Play account on your device for this App to our server; our server queries Google Play and stores the following record: purchase token, order number, product, validity dates and status (active, cancelled, refunded, etc.). Because there is no sign-in on the phone, the record is linked to an internal user number generated randomly by our server (it contains no email, name or Google account identifier); subscriptions bought with sign-in in earlier versions continue with their own account records. Your payment card details never reach us; they are handled directly by Google Play. You can cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play.

Your entitlement stays valid without an internet connection for at most 48 hours on the phone and at most 24 hours on the computer; after that the App connects to the internet to verify it again. When this time runs out your data is not deleted; the App only asks for an internet connection.

Free Trial Verification

On the phone, the free trial is only available in the mobile app and needs no sign-in: each phone gets a 7-day free trial once. To prevent the trial from being taken repeatedly by uninstalling and reinstalling the App, the device identifier provided by your operating system (Android ID) and the date it was first seen are stored. The desktop (Windows) version has its own separate 3-day free trial: each Google account and each computer gets it once. To prevent it from being taken repeatedly by creating new accounts on the same computer, a one-way hash of the computer's Windows identifier (MachineGuid) and the trial start/end dates are stored with the trial record. A trial started on the phone does not unlock the computer, and the computer trial does not unlock the phone.

In the October 2026 make-good campaign, each phone gets all features free for 15 days once; to prevent it being taken repeatedly, the server stores only a number derived from a one-way hash of the device identifier and the start/end dates (not the raw device identifier). For the 30 days added to paid access, the subscription's internal user number, a one-way hash of the purchase token and the old/new end dates are recorded.

Device Registration

To prevent a licence from being copied and used on several devices, each licence is valid on one device: the phone licence on one phone, the Windows licence on one computer. For this, the following record linked to your account is kept on the server: the registered phone's device identifier (Android ID), the registered computer's one-way hash, a one-way hash of the latest session token (not the token itself) and the date of the last "Reset my registered device" action. You can change the registered device once every 30 days; the session on the old device is closed. This record is deleted when you delete your account.

When the trial or subscription ends, the daily free-use counter in the mobile app is tied to your phone. The server keeps the device identifier, the day, the number of uses per feature and a one-way hash of the part whose result was shown that day (not the dimensions themselves); records older than 3 days are deleted.

On a computer without a licence, the right to see the result of a single part (sample calculation) is given once per computer. For this, the computer's one-way hash, the user number of the account that used it, a one-way hash of the part and the date of use are stored. To prevent the right from being taken again by deleting and re-creating an account, this record is not deleted when you delete your account.

Desktop Version (Windows and Linux)

You sign in to the desktop version with the same Google account; the phone licence and the Windows licence are separate, and so are the phone's 7-day trial and the computer's 3-day trial (see Free Trial Verification and Device Registration). The differences are:

  • Sign-in: Sign in with Google opens in your computer's default browser; after sign-in the browser returns only to the App on this computer (127.0.0.1). The session token is stored encrypted in your operating system's protected storage (DPAPI on Windows).
  • Payment — Stripe: The desktop licence is charged through Stripe (the phone licence is bought through Google Play on Android; the checkout page states which devices the package you buy covers). Stripe processes your payment and billing details (card, email, country); your card details never reach us. The subscription status is kept on our payment server running on Cloudflare (bendiq-api.ozerlabs.com), linked to your Google account, and contains only: Google account number, Stripe customer number, Stripe subscription number, plan (price code), status, validity date and Stripe notification records (notification number, type, date). Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy. You cancel the subscription and change the payment method in the Stripe customer portal.
  • Computer registration: The Windows licence is valid on one computer. For this, your computer's operating system identifier (Windows MachineGuid / Linux machine-id) is passed through a one-way hash on your computer; only this hash, the platform (Windows/Linux) and the first/last seen dates are sent to the server. The raw identifier never reaches us and cannot be recovered from the hash. If another computer is registered, the App tells you; you can reset the registered computer once every 30 days and register this one. When you delete your account, the device records are deleted too (except the sample calculation record, see Device Registration).
  • Files: PDF and DXF files are saved to the folder you choose through the operating system's "Save" dialog; printing uses the operating system's print dialog.
  • Microphone: The desktop version has no voice measurement input; the App does not request microphone permission.

Deleting Your Account

The phone version creates no account, so there is no sign-in identity to delete; you cancel your subscription in Google Play › Subscriptions. If you signed in with Google in an earlier phone version or in the desktop version, you can delete your account as follows.

In the desktop version you can delete your account inside the App: Settings → Delete account. Deletion removes your sign-in identity (email address and name) from our server. Purchase and trial records are kept without your email address for legal (tax/record-keeping) obligations, refund handling and to prevent abuse of the free trial; they are kept for 10 years and then deleted. Registered device details are deleted with the account; the computer sample calculation record is not deleted (see Device Registration). Local data on your device (dimensions, favourites) is deleted when you uninstall the App.

If you no longer have access to the App, send your deletion request to support@ozerlabs.com from the Google email address you signed in with, with the subject "BENDIQ account deletion"; it is processed the same way as in-app deletion.

AI Measurement Scan

When you use this feature, the profile photo you take or select is sent through a secure server (Supabase Edge Function) to Google's Gemini AI service to detect the corner points. The photo is used only for this analysis and is not stored permanently on the server. A general usage counter (IP-based, daily, to prevent abuse) is kept during this process; this counter does not contain your personal identity.

Voice Measurement Input

When you use the voice command feature, your speech is processed by your device's operating system / Google speech recognition service to convert it into text. This does not pass through our servers; voice data never reaches us.

Generated Files (PDF and DXF)

PDF technical drawings and DXF flat pattern files you create in the App are generated entirely on your device and are only handled by your device's share/save systems when you choose to share or save them. These files are never seen or stored by us.

Third-Party Services

The App uses only the following services: Supabase (desktop sign-in, subscription and trial verification, AI scan server) , Google (Sign in with Google — desktop only, Google Play Billing, Google Play Developer API, Gemini) and, on the desktop version, Stripe (payments) and Cloudflare (payment server). It shows no ads and uses no analytics/tracking software (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.).

Because the App is distributed and paid for through Google Play (and the desktop version uses Sign in with Google), Google's own privacy policy also applies: policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions

  • File system / sharing: to save and share PDF and DXF files. Used only at your request.
  • Microphone: used only when you start "Voice Measurement Input" and only while you are speaking. Recordings are never sent to our servers or saved on the device; they are passed to the operating system's speech recognition service and converted into text. If you do not grant the permission, the rest of the App works normally.
  • Camera / gallery: only to take or select a photo on the "AI Measurement Scan" screen. The App never takes photos in the background.
  • Internet: to verify Google Play purchases and the free trial, for AI scan and purchases.

The App does not access location, contacts, call history, SMS or similar data.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for professionals working in the sheet metal bending industry and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 13.
