BENDIQ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026  ·  Türkçe sürüm

Overview

BENDIQ (the "App") is a professional tool for sheet metal bending calculations. We take our users' privacy seriously. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the App.

Data We Collect

The dimensions you enter, material selections, saved favourites, company name and language preference are stored only in your device's local storage (localStorage) and are not sent to our servers.

Two features are the exception and are described separately below: AI Measurement Scan (photo processing) and In-App Purchase (PRO licence).

AI Measurement Scan

When you use this feature, the profile photo you take or select is sent, through a secure server (a Supabase Edge Function), to Google's Gemini AI service for the sole purpose of detecting corner points. The photo is used only for that analysis and is not stored permanently on the server. During this process a general usage counter is kept (IP-based, daily) to prevent abuse; this counter does not contain your personal identity.

In-App Purchase (PRO)

PRO licence purchases are processed through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. These providers process the transaction data required to complete the purchase (such as purchase identifier and device identifier) under their own privacy policies: RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Your payment card details never reach us; they are handled directly by Google Play.

Trial Period Verification

To prevent abuse of the 30-day free trial (gaining a new trial by uninstalling and reinstalling the App), an anonymous device identifier provided by your device's operating system (Android Device ID) is sent to our secure server and associated with the trial start date. This identifier is never matched with your personal information (name, e-mail, etc.).

Voice Measurement Input

When you use the voice command feature, your speech is processed by your device's operating system / Google's speech recognition service in order to be converted into text. This does not pass through our servers; the audio data never reaches us.

Generated Files (PDF)

The PDF technical drawings you create in the App are generated entirely on your device and are only processed through your device's sharing/saving systems when you choose to share or save them. These files are neither seen nor stored by us.

Third-Party Services

Apart from Supabase (AI scan and device verification), Google Gemini (AI scan) and Google Play Billing / RevenueCat (purchases) as described above, the App shows no advertising and uses no analytics or tracking software (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.).

Because the App is distributed through the Google Play Store, Google's own privacy policy also applies (for purchases, download statistics and similar): policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions

  • File system / sharing: to save and share PDF files. Used only at your request (when you press the create/share/print PDF buttons).
  • Microphone: used only when you start the "Voice Measurement Input" feature, and only while you are speaking. Audio is never sent to our servers and is not stored on the device; it is passed to the operating system's speech recognition service to be converted into text. If you decline this permission, the rest of the App works normally.
  • Camera / gallery: only to take or select a photo on the "AI Measurement Scan" screen. The App never takes photos on its own in the background.
  • Internet: for AI scanning, trial period verification and purchases.

The App does not access location, contacts, call history, SMS or any similar data.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for professionals working in the sheet metal bending industry and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 13 (no data is collected at all).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. This page will be updated when significant changes are made.
